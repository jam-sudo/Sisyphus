# Almitrine bismesylate dose-basis correction (2026-09-25)

The pinned Omega workbook (`approved.xlsx`, SHA256 `4521a2d89dde71c9e9381ab0a920e84dfc69c714860de377cd73b338572026d2`) records almitrine at 25, 50, 100, and 200 mg, with parent-plasma Cmax values of 38.9, 104.4, 204, and 286.2 ng/mL. The [original clinical study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2566337/) explicitly says the capsules contained those amounts of **almitrine bismesylate** and that plasma **almitrine** was measured. The fitted dataset used the parent [almitrine structure](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chebi/CHEBI:53778), but had treated the 25 mg salt dose as 25 mg parent.

[ChEBI's parent entry](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chebi/CHEBI:53778) gives average mass 477.563 g/mol, and its [dimesylate entry](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chebi/CHEBI:53779) identifies bismesylate as the same salt and gives 669.777 g/mol. Thus parent fraction = 477.563 / 669.777 = 0.713018; the four parent-equivalent doses are 17.8254, 35.6509, 71.3018, and 142.6036 mg. The measured parent Cmax and AUC values remain unchanged.

The guarded Cmax recipe now converts the 25 mg aggregate, and both exploratory MMPK tables convert all four arms. The stale PBPK-feature prediction for the old dose was removed. Rebuilding CL/F and Vd/F changes almitrine's derived values from 6.3784 to 4.5479 mL/min/kg and from 34.0286 to 24.2630 L/kg. The raw Omega source remains pinned. Fitted counts remain Cmax 906, CL/F 900, Vd/F 831.

After refitting, direct Cmax scaffold-CV AAFE changes from 3.3827 to **3.3932**. On the repeatedly accessed development N=73, direct ML AAFE changes from 2.9925 to **3.0024** and Meta AAFE from 2.8053 to **2.7965** (conditional bootstrap 95% CI **2.2672–3.4882**). The paired Meta/ML AAFE ratio is **0.9314** (CI **0.8232–1.0537**). The nominal 90% development-residual interval has **10.48-fold** half-width and covers **67/73** consumed labels. This repairs a source-to-model dose inconsistency; it is not independent accuracy evidence.
