# Cmax administered-drug/analyte identity screen (2026-09-24)

The shipped direct Cmax model takes a **parent structure and oral dose**. In the pinned [Omega source workbook](https://github.com/jam-sudo/Omega/blob/08a45047a2b5dcdca8c9a8f36ff1fe3b50ed3d6d/data/external/mmpk/approved.xlsx) (SHA256 `4521a2d89dde71c9e9381ab0a920e84dfc69c714860de377cd73b338572026d2`), column D identifies the measured analyte, column E the reported dose, column H Cmax, and column Q identifies a different administered precursor or drug for some arms. The [pinned Omega aggregate](https://github.com/jam-sudo/Omega/blob/08a45047a2b5dcdca8c9a8f36ff1fe3b50ed3d6d/data/ml/clinical/mmpk_clean.csv) names its row after the analyte. An analyte structure with the precursor's unadjudicated dose is not a verified parent-drug training example.

For every fitted Omega row, the audit grouped workbook arms by analyte name and dose, checked that the arm count equalled `n_studies`, and checked that the geometric mean of arm Cmax values matched the fitted aggregate. **114 aggregates** matched exactly and contained at least one arm whose column-Q identity differed from the analyte. The [row-level ledger](../../data/validation/cmax_administered_analyte_mismatch_v1.csv) records fitted values, source workbook row numbers, column-Q identities, and discordant-arm counts. It includes both fully discordant and mixed aggregates; for example, two of the three desvenlafaxine 50 mg arms name venlafaxine. The raw workbook and Omega CSV are preserved byte-for-byte, and the model recipe pins both the Omega SHA and the ledger SHA before removing those 114 rows.

One clear example is **mycophenolic acid 500 mg**: workbook rows 2655–2656 report 15,884.98 and 14,683.17 ng/mL mycophenolic acid after **mycophenolate mofetil** dosing. Their geometric mean is the fitted 15.2722579 mg/L. The [original study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20399993/) analyzes MMF and MPA separately; the corresponding parent-MMF measurements appear in workbook rows 2651–2652. Other ledger entries include acyclovir after valaciclovir, tenofovir after tenofovir disoproxil, and enantiomer measurements after racemate dosing. Some entries concern salts or other dose forms that may be recoverable with documented active-moiety conversion. The ledger is a **quarantine list, not a claim that all 114 observations are chemically wrong**. Reinstatement requires source-specific proof that the model input structure, oral dose basis, and measured parent analyte match.

One recoverable salt example has been identified, but remains excluded from the shipped fit: workbook row 3010 reports phenylbutyrate Cmax 218 mg/L after a 5,000 mg sodium phenylbutyrate tablet dose. The [FDA BUPHENYL label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/020572s025,020573s022lbl.pdf) independently specifies plasma phenylbutyrate Cmax 218 µg/mL after that single fasting dose. A free-acid phenylbutyrate structure would require a 4,409.7 mg active-moiety dose (5,000 × 164.204/186.186 by molecular weight), not the source row's unconverted 5,000 mg salt dose. This source check does not change the fitted dataset or its validation claims.

The 114 aggregates were removed from the fitted Cmax snapshot. To prevent exploratory model reuse before adjudication, 322 rows were removed from each of `mmpk_expanded_full.csv` and `mmpk_expanded_v2.csv`, and 114 from `mmpk_pbpk_features.csv`; the extra exploratory doses have not all been separately source-adjudicated. The original Omega CSV remains in the conservative training-membership registry, so removing fitted rows does not turn these identities into eligible external-holdout candidates. Historical out-of-fold outputs remain historical.

Together with the earlier [blood-matrix quarantine](development_immunosuppressant_training_matrix_2026-09-24.md), the fitted Cmax set at this checkpoint had **910** rows. Relative to the immediately preceding 1,024-row model, five-fold CV AAFE changed **3.2447 → 3.362**. On the repeatedly accessed N=73 development set, direct ML AAFE changed **3.3845 → 3.1230** and Meta AAFE **2.9033 → 2.8538**. The conditional paired Meta/ML ratio is **0.9138** (bootstrap 95% CI **0.8013–1.0396**). The score improvement on this consumed set is not independent validation; the CV degradation and remaining source uncertainties are reported alongside it. A subsequent [pimecrolimus blood-matrix quarantine](development_pimecrolimus_training_matrix_2026-09-24.md) reduced the fitted set to 909 rows.
