# Dapagliflozin development Cmax: original study table — 2026-09-24

The scored 10 mg dapagliflozin reference used **121.809 ng/mL**, the maximum
of an OSP-digitized *mean concentration-time profile*. A maximum of the mean
profile is not the mean (or geometric mean) of individual peak concentrations.

[Kasichayanula et al. 2011, Table 1](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1463-1326.2011.01397.x)
directly reports **136 ng/mL** as the **geometric mean of individual parent-plasma
Cmax** (CV 22%) after a single fasted oral **10 mg** dose in **14 healthy adults**.
The same table reports 94 ng/mL for the *fed* parent arm and 224 ng/mL for the
fasted glucuronide metabolite; neither is the matching outcome. The
[original article PDF](https://pillbuys.com/research/Dapagliflozin/7.pdf)
provides a readable Table 1. The source does not establish the post-dose meal
time or exact release formulation, so this development arm remains silver and
does not qualify for the external protocol's primary cohort on this evidence.

The scored parent Cmax is now **0.136 mg/L**. The OSP digitization remains an
archival input, while the approximate 165 ng/mL curated midpoint and 158 ng/mL
steady-state extraction are marked superseded so integration cannot restore a
different arm. No model or cohort membership changed. N=79 development Meta
AAFE moves **2.8940 → 2.8980**, with conditional compound-bootstrap 95% CI
**2.3757–3.6106**. This reference correction is neither a model regression
nor independent external validation.
