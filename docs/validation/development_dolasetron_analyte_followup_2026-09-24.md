# Dolasetron parent/analyte training correction (2026-09-24)

The pinned [Omega source workbook](https://github.com/jam-sudo/Omega/blob/08a45047a2b5dcdca8c9a8f36ff1fe3b50ed3d6d/data/external/mmpk/approved.xlsx) labels seven 50–400 mg oral dolasetron-mesylate-monohydrate arms (rows 1285–1291) as `dolasetron`. The selected 200 mg aggregate has Cmax 578.1 ng/mL. All seven rows cite [Boxenbaum et al. 1993, PMID 8453023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8453023/). That study reports that plasma **parent dolasetron was low and sporadic**, preventing parent pharmacokinetic analysis; the quantified, dose-related profile was for **reduced dolasetron (hydrodolasetron)**. The [FDA ANZEMET label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=201b0eb8-4a1e-4e22-ab4b-addb642710d6) separately identifies the 200 mg oral-dose Cmax table (556 ng/mL in its population) as plasma hydrodolasetron. The label's value differs from the workbook's 578.1 ng/mL and is corroboration of analyte identity, not a numerical replacement.

The shipped direct Cmax target is **parent plasma Cmax**. The fitted 200 mg row was therefore removed from `cmax_omega_public_clean.csv`; all seven dose rows were removed from each exploratory `mmpk_expanded_{full,v2}.csv`, and the derived `mmpk_pbpk_features.csv` row was removed. The same source's AUC and half-life had fed a dolasetron CL/F and Vd/F training row; because the paper says parent oral PK could not be analyzed, that row was also removed from `clf_training.csv` before retraining both models. The original Omega CSV and workbook remain byte-pinned in the conservative source inventory, and historical deconvolution output is retained as historical output. The model recipe checks the exact 200 mg aggregate before exclusion.

Fitted direct Cmax N changed **908 → 907**; its five-fold scaffold CV AAFE changed **3.3652 → 3.3240**. Fitted CL/F and Vd/F counts are **1009** and **936**; their five-fold row-CV R² values are **0.1973** and **0.3686**. On the repeatedly accessed N=73 development benchmark, ML AAFE changed **3.0343 → 3.1205** and Meta AAFE **2.8405 → 2.8544**. The conditional paired Meta/ML ratio is **0.9147** (bootstrap 95% CI **0.8050–1.0355**). The small development regression is retained because the prior label did not represent the target analyte. None of these numbers is independent external validation.
