# Givinostat paired-mass correction (2026-09-25)

The pinned Omega 2024 supplement records five fasted, single-dose ITF2357 capsule arms from [Furlan et al. 2011, Table 2](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.2119/molmed.2011.00020.pdf): 50–600 mg, with the 50 mg arm's parent-plasma Cmax **53 ng/mL** and **111 nmol/L**. Those two concentration units imply 477.5 g/mol; the paired AUC values **359 ng·h/mL** and **754 nmol·h/L** imply 476.1 g/mol. Rounding accounts for the difference from the hydrochloride-monohydrate molecular weight **475.97 g/mol** in the [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2024/217865Orig1s000lbl.pdf). They do not match the free-base mass. The same label explicitly equates 10 mg hydrochloride monohydrate with 8.86 mg givinostat.

The 2011 paper calls the administered capsules ITF2357 but does not state their salt-mass convention or calibration-standard composition. A later [Italfarmaco protocol for ITF2357 capsules, §4.4.1](https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/32/NCT01901432/Prot_001.pdf), states that ITF2357 means givinostat hydrochloride monohydrate and that its capsule doses and concentrations are expressed on that basis. Applying that sponsor convention to the earlier study is a **source-linked inference**, not direct inspection of the 2011 batch record. It is consistent with the original table's mass–mole conversion and its use of the same research code and capsule form. The conversion must be revisited if the 2011 formulation or assay-calibration record shows a different convention.

The fitted 50 mg/0.053 mg/L aggregate and all five exploratory dose arms now multiply **dose, Cmax, and AUC together** by 0.886. The raw Omega source remains unchanged. The dose-normalized Cmax target and derived CL/F and Vd/F are unchanged; a fresh Cmax retrain reproduced the **identical model SHA256** `9f208986df039551e8170fb90a1af883bcea87bc2d359d6d9ac65362a54ab011`, and rebuilding CL/F reproduced the identical training-table SHA256 `e8095564c47d2c7a114b7d963258326e20cfb06792119bb27d4559f00e11c75e`. The one archived PBPK-feature row was removed because its engine prediction used the old 50 mg dose. The development benchmark and interval are unchanged; this is unit-basis provenance work, not independent accuracy evidence.
