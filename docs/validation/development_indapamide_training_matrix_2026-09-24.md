# Indapamide Cmax training-matrix adjudication (2026-09-24)

The pinned [Omega Cmax snapshot](https://github.com/jam-sudo/Omega/blob/08a45047a2b5dcdca8c9a8f36ff1fe3b50ed3d6d/data/ml/clinical/mmpk_clean.csv) contains a 5 mg indapamide label of **0.2464812366 mg/L** (`n_studies=2`). Its [source workbook](https://github.com/jam-sudo/Omega/blob/08a45047a2b5dcdca8c9a8f36ff1fe3b50ed3d6d/data/external/mmpk/approved.xlsx), rows 1993–1994, identifies the two tablet arms as **263** and **231 ng/mL**, both attributed to [Grebow et al. 1982](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7106164/), DOI 10.1007/BF00548396. Their geometric mean is 246.481 ng/mL. The workbook's AUC values 4210 and 4670 ng·h/mL average to the exploratory row's 4440; the original paper explicitly calls these **blood** concentration–time profiles and reports AUC about 4.2 and 4.7 µg·h/mL. This is a whole-blood target, whereas the direct Cmax model predicts parent **plasma** concentration.

The original Omega file remains byte-pinned for provenance and conservative membership screening. The fitted-row recipe now checks the identity, dose, study count, and exact source-derived Cmax before excluding this one aggregate. It does **not** divide the blood value by an assumed partition factor or replace it with the development reference. The separate [Li et al. 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23447043/) 5 mg reference arm measured parent **plasma** Cmax **47.79 ng/mL**; it remains a development reference, not a replacement training label.

The two 2.5 mg entries in the exploratory `mmpk_expanded_{full,v2}.csv` files combine 98 and 113.6 ng/mL source-workbook arms. The [2010 source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20712133/) explicitly measures whole blood for the 113.6 ng/mL arm; the 2000 arm's matrix has not been separately rechecked. Both exploratory 2.5 mg rows, both 5 mg rows, and the derived `mmpk_pbpk_features.csv` 5 mg aggregate were removed pending plasma-specific source adjudication. Historical OOF predictions are retained as historical outputs, not fitted input to the shipped model.

After retraining on **1,027** fitted rows, five-fold Cmax CV AAFE changed **3.2371 → 3.2584**. The repeatedly accessed N=73 development benchmark changed ML AAFE **3.3773 → 3.1917** and Meta AAFE **2.8846 → 2.8468**; the paired Meta/ML AAFE ratio is **0.8919** (bootstrap 95% CI **0.7810–1.0115**). The CV result worsened slightly, while development metrics improved; none is independent external validation. The source, fitted dataset, model, benchmark, interval, and display artifacts are hash-pinned or regenerated in this change.
