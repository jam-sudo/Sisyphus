# Methenamine label quarantine (2026-09-24)

The pinned Omega `approved.xlsx` (SHA256 `4521a2d89dde71c9e9381ab0a920e84dfc69c714860de377cd73b338572026d2`, rows 2449–2450) pairs two 1,000 mg **methenamine hippurate** arms with serum peaks of 29.8 and 30.7 mg/L, AUCs of 168 and 163 mg·h/L, and CL/F values of 5.64 and 5.93 L/h. The fitted aggregate used parent-methenamine SMILES and the geometric-mean Cmax 30.2467 mg/L. The [original article's abstract](https://academic.oup.com/jac/article-abstract/9/3/209/766298) confirms two 1 g hippurate formulations, a methenamine serum assay, total clearance about 1.58 mL/min/kg, and distribution volume about 0.56 L/kg. The [HIPREX FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2017/017681s018lbl.pdf) confirms that 1 g is **salt**, not parent, mass.

Pure molecular-weight arithmetic would convert 1,000 mg salt to `1,000 × 140.1863 / 319.36 = 438.9601 mg` parent ([NIST parent weight](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C100970&Mask=FFFF&Units=CAL), [PubChem salt weight](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/21945)). We initially made that conversion while retaining Cmax. A supervised, read-only Claude Code source challenge caught the inconsistency: `5.64 L/h × 168 mg·h/L = 947 mg`, near the study's **1 g** basis. Dividing 5.64 L/h and 37.21 L by the workbook's 61 kg gives 1.54 mL/min/kg and 0.61 L/kg, also near the article's abstract. Converting dose alone instead makes derived CL/F and Vd/F about 2.28-fold lower than the paper's values. A 439 mg parent dose in a 0.56 L/kg one-compartment volume cannot give the reported ~30 mg/L serum Cmax even at F=1; its instantaneous upper bound is about 12.9 mg/L.

The likely ambiguity is whether the assay concentrations and AUC were expressed as salt equivalents while named as methenamine, or whether the article's clearance calculations used salt mass with parent concentrations. The full numeric table was inaccessible, so neither interpretation is proven. **We therefore removed methenamine from fitted Cmax, CL/F, and Vd/F training and from the exploratory MMPK rows; the raw Omega workbook and CSV remain unchanged.** The archived PBPK-feature row was removed because its engine prediction used the unadjudicated dose. Reintroduction requires the original table, assay calibration basis, and dose/Cmax/AUC/CL mass balance to be checked together. This AI-assisted source audit is not a two-person curation or an independent external accuracy test.

After rebuilding, direct Cmax fits **906** drugs (scaffold-CV AAFE **3.3699**); CL/F and Vd/F fit **900** and **831** (row-CV R² **0.2181** and **0.3497**). On the repeatedly used development N=73, direct ML AAFE is **2.9848** and Meta AAFE **2.8158** (conditional bootstrap 95% CI **2.2881–3.5022**); the paired Meta/ML ratio is **0.9434** (CI **0.8319–1.0695**). The nominal 90% development-residual half-width is **9.61-fold** with **66/73** consumed-label coverage. These are version bookkeeping, not external performance evidence.
