# Omega Cmax upstream workbook coverage (2026-09-24)

The fitted Cmax snapshot is derived from the pinned [Omega aggregate CSV](https://github.com/jam-sudo/Omega/blob/08a45047a2b5dcdca8c9a8f36ff1fe3b50ed3d6d/data/ml/clinical/mmpk_clean.csv), but that aggregate has **two** upstream workbooks. The [original workbook](https://github.com/jam-sudo/Omega/blob/08a45047a2b5dcdca8c9a8f36ff1fe3b50ed3d6d/data/external/mmpk/approved.xlsx) has SHA256 `4521a2d89dde71c9e9381ab0a920e84dfc69c714860de377cd73b338572026d2`; the [2024 supplement](https://github.com/jam-sudo/Omega/blob/08a45047a2b5dcdca8c9a8f36ff1fe3b50ed3d6d/data/external/mmpk/approved_2024.xlsx) has SHA256 `bfdc1bac564dcf3a29f2f8021f69c4f6e087a687bea329433ccd47d233b4d32f`. The model metadata now names both immutable sources. The aggregate CSV remains the actual retraining input; the workbooks are provenance sources, not files required at inference time.

An exact-name, dose, arm-count, and geometric-Cmax reconciliation found **891** of the current 906 fitted aggregates in the original workbook and **six** unchanged in the supplement; two more supplemental aggregates have source-adjudicated mass conversions. Six more original-workbook aggregates retain their Cmax but use source-verified parent-equivalent doses: [lisdexamfetamine and zofenopril](development_salt_dose_and_clf_screen_followup_2026-09-24.md), then [metformin, bupropion, pyridostigmine, and trospium](development_additional_salt_dose_followup_2026-09-24.md). The remaining **felbamate** row is deliberately corrected from the original workbook's unit error, as [adjudicated separately](development_felbamate_source_correction_2026-09-24.md). The original-workbook count fell by one when the [dolasetron active-metabolite aggregate](development_dolasetron_analyte_followup_2026-09-24.md) was quarantined and by one more when the [methenamine concentration-basis label](development_methenamine_label_quarantine_2026-09-24.md) was quarantined. The 2024 supplement contains a ninth identity, elafibranor, which is not in the current fitted snapshot. The [seladelpar source poster](https://www.postersessiononline.eu/173580348_eu/congresos/ILC2019/aula/-FRI_41_ILC2019.pdf) independently displays the normal-function cohort's 71.9 ng/mL Cmax. Acoramidis's workbook 50 mg is **hydrochloride mass**; its fitted parent-equivalent dose is 44.4277 mg, as [adjudicated from PMDA and the original PK table](development_acoramidis_dose_basis_2026-09-25.md). Givinostat's workbook 50 mg and 53 ng/mL were [paired-converted to parent mass](development_givinostat_mass_basis_2026-09-25.md), yielding fitted 44.3 mg and 0.046958 mg/L. The 2011 capsule dose basis is inferred from the original table and a later sponsor protocol; its batch and calibration records remain unavailable. This workbook audit establishes upstream row coverage, not independent adjudication of every study's population, formulation, dose basis, or plasma matrix.
