# Parent-dose and CL/F source-screen follow-up (2026-09-24)

An independent Claude Code **AI-assisted source check**, separate from model editing, reviewed eight high-risk fitted Omega Cmax names against the pinned `approved.xlsx` (SHA256 `4521a2d89dde71c9e9381ab0a920e84dfc69c714860de377cd73b338572026d2`). It confirmed two **dose-basis** errors, not parent/metabolite Cmax substitutions. Five other names remained unresolved because their original numeric tables were inaccessible; terfenadine had one source arm directly corroborated. This check is not a two-human-curator adjudication or an external accuracy test.

- The selected lisdexamfetamine row used 30 mg dimesylate with parent lisdexamfetamine plasma Cmax 15.7 ng/mL. [The original study, Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4949011/), separately identifies the parent analyte; the [FDA VYVANSE label, section 11](https://www.fda.gov/media/142073/download) identifies a 30 mg dimesylate capsule as approximately 17.3 mg lisdexamfetamine. The fitted and seven exploratory dose rows now use the exact parent/dimesylate molecular-weight ratio `263.385/455.60`; their Cmax and AUC measurements are unchanged.
- The zofenopril row used 60 mg zofenopril calcium. [The cited primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10635443/) identifies the calcium salt and separate parent/metabolite plasma assays; the [Irish regulator product information, section 2](https://assets.hpra.ie/products/Human/22407/Licence_PA0865-003-004_07102019123150.pdf) identifies 60 mg calcium as 57.3 mg zofenopril. Its fitted and exploratory doses now use 57.3 mg parent. The original 91.16 ng/mL Cmax could not be checked in the paywalled numeric table and remains unverified.

The direct Cmax fit remains **N=907**; its five-fold scaffold CV AAFE is **3.3382**. The two old-dose rows were removed from the archived PBPK-feature snapshot because their stored engine predictions were calculated at the old doses.

More seriously, `clf_training.csv` had been left behind when the Cmax source was screened. Rebuilding it from the already screened `mmpk_expanded_full.csv` removed 120 stale rows: the 114 administered-drug/analyte mismatches in `cmax_administered_analyte_mismatch_v1.csv` and six blood-matrix rows. It also recomputed lisdexamfetamine and zofenopril CL/F and Vd/F from parent-equivalent doses. No other retained row changed. The table now has **1,007 drug rows**; the in-range CL/F and Vd/F fits use **901** and **832** rows, with five-fold row-CV R² **0.2060** and **0.3439**, respectively. Those CV metrics are internal, not external performance estimates.

After retraining and regenerating the repeatedly accessed development benchmark (N=73), direct ML AAFE is **3.0737** and Meta AAFE **2.8546** (conditional bootstrap 95% CI **2.3270–3.5539**); the paired Meta/ML ratio is **0.9287** (CI **0.8167–1.0530**). The nominal 90% development-residual interval has a **10.20-fold** multiplicative half-width and covers **66/73** consumed development compounds. None of these figures establish independent generalization. The 85-point readiness goal still requires untouched source-verified external testing and independent curation.
