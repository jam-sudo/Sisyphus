# Voclosporin Cmax training-matrix adjudication (2026-09-24)

The pinned [Omega source workbook](https://github.com/jam-sudo/Omega/blob/08a45047a2b5dcdca8c9a8f36ff1fe3b50ed3d6d/data/external/mmpk/approved.xlsx) records voclosporin oral-solution doses and Cmax values in rows 4295–4299: 0.25 mg/kg (18.07 mg at the source's body weight), 32 ng/mL; 0.5 mg/kg, 77 ng/mL; 1.5 mg/kg, 459 ng/mL; 3 mg/kg, 874.9 ng/mL; and 4.5 mg/kg, 955.5 ng/mL. Its selected [Omega Cmax aggregate](https://github.com/jam-sudo/Omega/blob/08a45047a2b5dcdca8c9a8f36ff1fe3b50ed3d6d/data/ml/clinical/mmpk_clean.csv) is 0.032 mg/L at 18.07 mg (`n_studies=1`). The [FDA review of study ISA03-10](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2021/213716Orig1s000MultidisciplineR.pdf), Table 77, independently reproduces all five Cmax values and dose levels. The [EMA assessment of the same study](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/withdrawal-report/withdrawal-assessment-report-luveniq_en.pdf) explicitly describes its dose-related concentrations, Cmax, and AUC as **whole blood**. The [original study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23736966/) identifies the single-ascending-dose oral regimen.

These are whole-blood parent-drug labels, while the shipped Cmax model targets plasma. The fitted 18.07 mg aggregate, all five exploratory dose rows in each of `mmpk_expanded_{full,v2}.csv`, and the derived `mmpk_pbpk_features.csv` row were quarantined. The original Omega CSV stays byte-pinned for provenance and conservative training-membership screening. No blood-to-plasma conversion was inferred. This is a source-arm adjudication, not a drug-class exclusion.

After this one-row fitted exclusion, Cmax training has **908** rows. The former five-fold row CV AAFE changed **3.359 → 3.369**. The training recipe now reports a five-fold Murcko-scaffold CV AAFE of **3.365** (R² **0.366**; 649 distinct scaffolds); the fitted production model hash did not change when only the CV partition changed. On the repeatedly accessed N=73 development benchmark, direct ML AAFE changed **3.0418 → 3.0343** and Meta AAFE **2.8496 → 2.8405**; the conditional paired Meta/ML ratio is **0.9361** (bootstrap 95% CI **0.8266–1.0569**). These diagnostics are not independent external validation.
